What is a pop-up event?

A one-time experience built around a single moment: a watch party, product launch, themed activation or brand experience.

Can you find the venue?

Yes. I scout and hold venues, and check permits for outdoor or public spaces.

How far ahead should I book?

Two to four months gives time for concept, custom pieces and permits.

Do you handle setup and teardown?

Yes. Load-in, build, running the event and load-out are all included.
