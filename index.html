How do I book my date?

Send me your details with the form, by text or by email. I'll send a tailored quote, and a signed agreement plus a 50% deposit locks in your date. The balance is due 14 days before your event.

What's the difference between day-of and month-of coordination?

Day-of: you've done the planning, and I take over about a week out to confirm details and run your event day. Month-of: I step in 4 to 6 weeks out to finalize logistics, confirm every vendor, build the timeline and own the run of show.

Is the deposit refundable?

The deposit holds your date and is non-refundable, because I turn away other events to keep it for you. If I'm ever unable to be there, I'll provide a qualified coordinator you approve, or refund everything you've paid.

Do you book vendors for me?

I'm happy to recommend vendors I trust, and with vendor sourcing and booking I'll find and book them for you. You sign with and pay each vendor directly, so you always know exactly what you're paying.

Can I change my date?

Yes. You can move your date once at no charge if you ask more than 30 days before the event and the new date is within 12 months and open on my calendar. Your payments move with you.

What areas do you serve?

All of Dallas–Fort Worth, including Dallas, Fort Worth, Keller, Southlake, Grapevine, Frisco, Plano and Arlington. Travel within DFW is included. Events more than 60 miles out add a travel fee.

Do you provide alcohol or bartending?

I don't serve or provide alcohol. Your caterer or venue's licensed, insured bartender handles that, and I'll coordinate them with the rest of your timeline.
