Questions

Fort Worth wedding FAQ

How much does a day-of wedding coordinator cost?

Day-of coordination starts at $1,000 and includes up to 12 hours on site, your minute-by-minute timeline and vendor confirmations. Extra hours are $75, rehearsal management is $250 and a second coordinator is $250.

Do you travel to venues outside Fort Worth?

Yes. I cover all of Dallas–Fort Worth with travel included. Venues more than 60 miles out add a travel fee.

When should I book my Fort Worth wedding coordinator?

Most couples book 3 to 9 months out. Peak spring and fall Saturdays go first, so reach out as soon as you have a date and venue.
