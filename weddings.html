When should I book a day-of wedding coordinator?

Book 3 to 9 months before your wedding. Popular spring and fall Saturdays in DFW fill up early. Under 30 days adds a 15% rush fee.

What does a day-of coordinator do?

I build your timeline, confirm every vendor, run the ceremony lineup and reception cues, set up your decor and handle anything that goes wrong, so you don't have to.

Do you run the rehearsal?

Yes, rehearsal management is a $250 add-on. I run the processional, hand out the order and collect personal items the night before.

Do you coordinate weddings outside DFW?

Travel anywhere in Dallas–Fort Worth is included. Weddings more than 60 miles out add a travel fee.
